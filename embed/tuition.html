What to Do

1

Watch for the application window. The state is expected to open 2027-28 applications in early 2027.

2

Apply at chooseact.alabama.gov for every child who will attend St. Bernard Prep, including full-tuition families. Families who already receive CHOOSE Act Alabama funds must apply again each year.

3

Need school aid beyond CHOOSE Act Alabama? Also apply through FACTS. Tuition forgiveness requires both applications.

4

Get help if you want it. Application help nights begin in January 2027 and continue as long as needed; dates to follow. Personal appointments are also available.

5

After an award, CHOOSE Act Alabama funds are paid to St. Bernard Prep through ClassWallet. We will walk you through it.

Common Questions

We pay full tuition. Do we still need to apply?

Yes. Without applying for CHOOSE Act Alabama, published tuition applies: $16,500 for high school and $11,850 for middle school. With CHOOSE Act Alabama, you pay $9,500 or $4,850.

Does the CHOOSE Act Alabama money come to our family?

No. It goes to St. Bernard Prep through ClassWallet, toward your child’s tuition.
